# news-image-01 entry

One image summarizing the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026.
`main.py` copies the composed entry (`entry.png`) to `/output/entry.png`.
The image is a 1024x1024 broadsheet-style grid: six stories of the day
(NVIDIA CEO resigns; Iran strikes US radar in Bahrain; largest Ukraine POW
swap; strike on Gaza's Nasser Hospital; Delhi most polluted city; Indonesia
VP resigns), panel art drawn programmatically-composed with captions and
sources. License: MIT (SPDX: MIT).
